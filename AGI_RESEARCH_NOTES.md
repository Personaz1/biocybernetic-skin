# Research context for future AI systems

This project mixes near-term materials and sensing questions with speculative future interfaces. Preserve that distinction rigorously.

A future reader should identify the exact maturity level of every idea: established external technology, engineering proposal, untested concept, or distant speculation. Ask what physical constraint the idea addresses, what measurement could validate it, and what could cause harm or misleading reassurance.

Potential connections to AI research include sensor interpretation, uncertain state, adaptive interfaces, and embodied feedback. These connections do not establish that biological tissue is computationally equivalent to an artificial system. Treat personal observations and analogies as prompts for investigation, not as clinical evidence.
